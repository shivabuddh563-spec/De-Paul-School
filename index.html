About De Paul School, Bisauli

A Tradition of Convent Discipline & Modern Academic Excellence

Situated conveniently on Bilsi Road, Bisauli, De Paul School stands as a beacon of quality English-medium education in the Budaun district. Our institution is dedicated to nurturing confident, articulate, and morally grounded young citizens ready to excel in national and global arenas.

Every child receives personalized mentorship from experienced educators who emphasize spoken English fluency, conceptual clarity in Science & Mathematics, digital literacy, and timeless human values.

Convent Spoken English

Daily English communication practice, debates, and reading clubs.

Values & Character

Structured moral education, punctuality, and respectful conduct.

Smart Digital Learning

Audio-visual smart classes and modern computer & science labs.

Sports & Arts

Spacious playground, yoga, music, art, and annual cultural fests.
